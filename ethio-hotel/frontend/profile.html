Our Story

Welcome to Ethio Hotel, where traditional Ethiopian hospitality meets modern luxury. Since our founding, we have been dedicated to providing an unforgettable experience for both business and leisure travelers. Nestled in the vibrant Bole district of Addis Ababa, our hotel offers stunning panoramic views of the city skyline and is just minutes away from the international airport.

Our rooms are meticulously designed to blend comfort with elegance, featuring premium linens, high-speed Wi-Fi, and 24/7 room service. Whether you are here for a quick layover or an extended vacation, our dedicated staff is committed to making your stay as comfortable and memorable as possible. Experience the rich flavors of our local cuisine, relax by our rooftop pool, and let us take care of the details while you focus on what matters most.
