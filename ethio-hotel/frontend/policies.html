Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Ethio Hotel, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Info: Name, email address, phone number, and billing details when you make a booking.
  • Payment Data: We use secure third-party processors (Chapa, Telebirr). We do not store your full card or bank details on our servers.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited to improve our website experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to process your bookings, communicate with you about your stay, and send important updates or promotional offers (only if you opt-in).

3. Data Security

All data is stored securely using encrypted MongoDB databases. We enforce strict access controls, meaning only authorized hotel staff can access guest information when necessary.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share necessary details with our payment processing partners and legal authorities if required by Ethiopian law.

1. Booking & Payment

  • All bookings are subject to room availability.
  • Prices are listed in Ethiopian Birr (ETB) and include applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.
  • Full payment is required at the time of booking for online reservations.

2. Guest Responsibilities

Guests are responsible for any damage caused to hotel property during their stay. Valid ID is required at check-in.

3. Check-In / Check-Out

  • Check-In: 2:00 PM onwards

4. Limitation of Liability

Ethio Hotel is not liable for any loss of personal belongings or valuables left in rooms. Please use the provided in-room safes.

Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We understand that plans change. Our cancellation policy is designed to be fair to both our guests and our business.

Standard Cancellation

  • More than 48 hours before check-in: Full refund (100%).
  • 24 to 48 hours before check-in: 50% refund.
  • Less than 24 hours before check-in: No refund.

Non-Refundable Bookings

Certain promotional rates or special packages are marked as non-refundable and cannot be cancelled for a refund.

How to Cancel

You can cancel your booking directly from your Guest Dashboard by clicking the "Cancel" button next to your booking, or by contacting our front desk.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method (Chapa/Telebirr) within 5-7 business days.
